6AM Advisory Limited — Secretarial Templates
============================================
Folder: Templates\Secretarial\
Firm: 6AM Advisory Limited
Registered office (for RO change templates):
  Unit 7, 20/F, Boss Commercial Centre, 28 Ferry Street, Yau Ma Tei, Kowloon, Hong Kong

Formatting: Times New Roman 11; ~2cm margins; fill-in placeholders like [Company Name (English)], [Date], [BR No.], [Director Name].
No CoSecHK merge fields (${...} / LOOP / MORE / GET).

WHEN TO USE
-----------

Board Meeting Minutes.docx
  Ordinary board meeting minutes (standalone). Use for general board resolutions not covered by a specialised pack.

AGM Pack - Notice Minutes Consent.docx
  Annual general meeting pack: notice of AGM, AGM minutes, and consent to short notice / related member documents.

Board Minutes - Change of Bank Account Company Name.docx
  Board minutes specifically for changing bank account particulars and/or company name (banking / naming resolutions).

Bought and Sold Note.docx
  Standalone bought and sold note for share transfers (stamp duty).

Instrument of Transfer.docx
  Standalone instrument of transfer of shares.

Instrument of Transfer and Bought Sold Note.docx
  Combined instrument of transfer + bought and sold note in one file (common for a single transfer filing set).

Confirmation - No SPA by Transferor.docx
  Transferor confirmation that there is no sale and purchase agreement (SPA) relating to the transfer.

Written Confirmation to IRD.docx
  Written confirmation letter to the Inland Revenue Department in connection with share transfer / stamp duty matters.

Change of CS and Address.docx
  Documents for change of company secretary and/or registered office address (use firm name and firm RO address above where the CS/service provider or new RO is 6AM).

Director Resolution - Share Transfer.docx
  Directors' written / board resolution approving a share transfer and related registrations.

EGM Minutes - Application for Deregistration.docx
  EGM (or members') minutes approving application for deregistration of the company.

Resignation Letter - CS or Director.docx
  Resignation letter template for a company secretary or a director.

SCR Notice - Significant Controllers.docx
  Notice / documentation relating to the significant controllers register (SCR).

Secretarial Service Agreement.docx
  Engagement letter / secretarial service agreement between 6AM Advisory Limited and the client company.

Company Information Form.docx
  Intake / KYC-style company information form for opening or updating a secretarial file.

Special Resolution - Change of Company Name.docx
  Members' special resolution to change the company name.

Special Resolution - Company Become Dormant.docx
  Members' special resolution for the company to become dormant.

Special Resolution - Dispense with AGM.docx
  Members' special resolution to dispense with holding annual general meetings (where applicable).

Special Resolution - FS without Business Review.docx
  Members' special resolution to approve financial statements without a business review (where applicable).

Share Transfer Confirmation - No Business Commenced.docx
  Confirmation that the company has not commenced business (often used with share transfer / stamp duty packs).

Share Transfer Confirmation - No SPA No Property.docx
  Confirmation of no SPA and no Hong Kong immovable property (share transfer / stamp duty support).

Written Resolutions - Directors.docx
  Generic directors' written resolutions template.

Articles of Association - English.docx
  English Model Articles (Cap. 622H style). All CoSecHK merge fields cleared → [placeholders].
  Fill company name, BR, incorporation date, share capital, and founder rows; delete unused founder rows.

Articles of Association - Chinese Sample A.docx
  Chinese Sample A. Same placeholder clean-up as English. Adapt per client; not a full custom redraft.


Written Resolutions - First Directors.docx
  Post-incorporation first directors' written resolutions: note CI, appoint CS (6AM Advisory Limited optional), fix RO, allot/issue founder shares, authorise share certificates, open SCR, note ARD, authorise bank account opening, Common Seal (if any).

Written Resolutions - First Shareholders.docx
  First members' written resolutions: confirm AoA, appoint first directors, approve CS appointment, approve RO, approve share capital/allotment as applicable.

Share Certificate.docx
  Classic HK private company share certificate (cert no., company, shareholder, number/class, consideration/paid, date, director + CS signatures / Common Seal placeholder).

Consent to Act as Director.docx
  Ongoing consent to act as director (not NNC1/NNC3 PDF): name, ID/passport, address, nationality, Cap.622 consent, signature/date; optional corporate director block.

Board Minutes - Allotment of Shares.docx
  Board minutes to allot new shares, update Register of Members, issue certificates, authorise NSC1 filing.

Share Application Form.docx
  Simple application to the company for allotment of shares (applicant particulars and consideration).

Ordinary Resolution - Removal of Auditor.docx
  Members' ordinary resolution removing the auditor; authorise NA1 filing and related notices.

Written Resolutions - Appointment of Auditor.docx
  Board and/or members' resolutions appointing an auditor (fill auditor name/address); delete unused block.

Notice of Intention to Remove Auditor.docx
  Cap.622-style notice to auditor and members of intended removal (use with NA1 pack).

Ordinary Resolution - Removal of Director.docx
  Members' ordinary resolution removing a director; authorise ND2A filing. Observe special notice / representations under Cap.622.

Special Resolution - Alteration of Articles.docx
  Members' special resolution to alter/adopt Articles of Association; authorise NAA1 filing; attach new AA by reference.

Special Resolution - Alteration of Objects.docx
  Members' special resolution to alter company objects; authorise NAA2 where relevant.

Resolutions - Share Buy-back or Capital Alteration.docx
  Modular pack (delete unused sections): board/member resolutions for share buy-back/redemption, alteration of share capital, variation of class rights; NSC form placeholders; solvency statement reference note (no IRD content).

Proxy Form.docx
  AGM/EGM/general meeting proxy form for Cap.622 private company.

Written Resolutions - Non-HK Company Filings.docx
  Supporting board resolutions for registered non-HK companies: appoint/change authorised representative, change HK address, change directors/CS particulars for NN filings, authorise filings.

Consent to Act as Company Secretary.docx
  Consent to act as company secretary - default block for 6AM Advisory Limited; alternative individual CS block.

HOW TO FILL
-----------
1. Copy the template into the company folder (usually 01_Constitution / 07_Correspondence / 03_CR_Filings as appropriate).
2. Replace every [bracket] with real data; delete unused signature / founder lines.
3. For Change of CS / RO: new CS = 6AM Advisory Limited; new RO = firm address above (unless client keeps own address).
