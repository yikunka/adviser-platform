AMLO Hit Disposition Memo
=========================
File: templates/AMLO_Hit_Disposition_Memo.docx
Rebuild: node scripts/build-amlo-hit-memo.mjs

Purpose
-------
Word memo for AMLO officers when a client/company screening produces a PEP / AML / FATF hit.
Clarify the situation, attach supporting evidence, and record one final disposition.

Final dispositions (align with AMLAlertResolution)
--------------------------------------------------
1. FALSE_POSITIVE — 誤報; continue CDD / relationship
2. ESCALATED — start Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD); not a reject
3. REJECTED — terminate / refuse; do NOT start EDD

REJECTED follow-up actions (checklist in the Word file)
-------------------------------------------------------
- 停止开户 / 不提供服务
- 终止现有业务关系（如已建立）
- 书面通知客户（如适用）
- 按政策评估是否另需联络 JFIU

Usage
-----
1. Download / open AMLO_Hit_Disposition_Memo.docx
2. Complete subject, hit summary, analysis, and supporting annex list
3. Tick exactly one disposition and obtain AMLO sign-off
4. Upload the signed PDF/JPG/PNG as supporting evidence when resolving the alert in /admin/aml
